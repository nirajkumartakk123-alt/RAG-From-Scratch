Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI models designed to understand and generate human-like text.
They are trained on massive amounts of textual data to learn language patterns, context, and relationships between words.
LLMs can perform tasks such as answering questions, summarizing text, translating languages, and generating code.
Popular examples include GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama.
They are widely used in chatbots, content generation, coding assistants, and AI-powered applications.
LLMs form the foundation of many modern Generative AI and agentic AI systems.
